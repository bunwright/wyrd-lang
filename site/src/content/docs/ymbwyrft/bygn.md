Wyrd is lȳtel fremmed sprǣc mid þrīm hēafoddǣlum. Ælc dǣl is on Yanxu awriten and mæg sundor bēon fandod.

```text
.wyrd bōc
    │
    ▼
stæfrǣdere ──► tācnu mid rǣw and stefn
    │
    ▼
rǣwere ──────► dǣdtrēow
    │
    ▼
fremmere ────► gield, gebind, and andswaru
```

## Þā bēc

| Bōc | Weorc |
| --- | --- |
| `src/词法.yx` | Unicode stæfrǣdere, word, tācnu, stōw |
| `src/语法.yx` | fore-rǣd rǣwere, strangnys, dǣdtrēow |
| `src/求值.yx` | lēafscop, cræftas, gield, fremming |
| `src/wyrd.yx` | open Wyrd ingang for ōþre Yanxu bēc |

## Stæfrǣdere

Se stæfrǣdere farþ þurh Unicode stafu and bereþ rǣw and stefn on ælcum tācne. Hē underfēhþ þrēo wordgemǣru—`"…"`, `“…”`, and `「…」`—and twēgen gemyndhādas, `#` and `//`.

## Rǣwere

Se rǣwere is handgewriten fore-rǣd rǣwere. Hē byġþ dǣdtrēow and fandaþ flōwgemǣru, swā `ġield` būtan cræfte oþþe `breċ` būtan ymbhwyrfte.

## Fremmere

Se fremmere brȳcþ gecnutene lēafscopas. Cræft gehealdaþ þone lēafscop þær hīe wurdon gesceapene; þæt ġiefþ beclysed gebind and eftclipung.

## Mihta

`言序.toml` belȳcþ net, process, natiue geīecnes, GUI, clipbord, and systembod. Se bēodrǣw hæfþ filmiht āna wiþ þæt nīehste bōchord.
