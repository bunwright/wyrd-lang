title: Bēodrǣw
description: Fremme, fand, and asc Wyrd fram þǣre bēodrǣwe.
---

Se Wyrd ingang is `src/主.yx`. Yanxu ġiefþ him þā æfterfylgendan word æfter `--`.

## Bōc fremman

```sh
yanxu 行 src/主.yx -- path/to/boc.wyrd
```

Wyrd rǣt, rǣwþ, and fremmeþ þā bōc. Sēo bōc hæfþ āna lēaf tō þǣm nīehstan bōchorde; net, process, GUI, and ōþre mihta sind belocene.

## Bōc fandian

```sh
yanxu 行 src/主.yx -- fand path/to/boc.wyrd
```

`fand` fremmeþ þone stæfrǣdere and rǣwere, ac ne fremmeþ þā dǣda. Riht bōc andswaraþ:

```text
Þēos bōc is riht.
```

## Fultum and gearwung

```sh
yanxu 行 src/主.yx -- fultum
yanxu 行 src/主.yx -- gearwung
```

Þā Englisc-līcan fana `--help` and `--version` weorcaþ swā gelīce.

## Gedwolancwide

Wyrd gedwolan habbaþ fæstne cȳþstaf and stōw `rǣw:stefn`:

```text
[FREM002] Se nama 'tungol' is fæst æt 3:1
```

| Fruma | Dǣl |
| --- | --- |
| `STAF` | stæfrǣdere and word |
| `RÆW` | rǣwere and bygning |
| `FREM` | fremmere and gield |
| `BEOD` | bēodrǣw and gield |

Þā cȳþstafas sind fæste innan þǣre 0.1 lǣstunge and magon on fandungum bēon gebruċene.
