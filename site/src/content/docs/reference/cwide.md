## Stæfrǣd

Cȳþword sind stafriht: `gield` nis `ġield`, and `thonne` nis `þonne`. Wyrd bēc sceolan UTF-8 bēon.

:::tip[Stafas]
On macOS: heald `Option` for macron and æ. On Linux: set þīnne Compose key. On ælcum ymbhwyrfte meaht þū þā word of þisse bēc ġeæfendlǣcan.
:::
